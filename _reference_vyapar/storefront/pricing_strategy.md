# VyaparPe Pricing Strategy & Plans

This document outlines the complete 3-tier hybrid pricing model (Monthly Subscription vs. Lifetime Ownership) designed for maximum profitability and customer acquisition.

---

## 1. BASIC PLAN (The Starter Edition)
*Target Audience:* New sellers, small Instagram stores, and local shops starting their online journey.

### Pricing Options:
- **Monthly Subscription:** ₹999 / month
- **Lifetime Ownership:** ₹15,000 (One-time) + ₹2,500 / year (Server & Maintenance Fee)

### Order Limits & Commission:
- **Free Orders:** Up to 500 Orders per month are absolutely FREE (0% Commission).
- **Post-Limit Commission:** 2% flat commission on every order after the first 500 orders in a month.

### Features Included:
- Standard E-commerce Website (Mobile & Desktop Responsive)
- Complete Admin Dashboard (Inventory, Orders, Customers)
- Payment Gateway Integration (Razorpay, PhonePe, UPI)
- Basic Email Support

### 🎁 Lifetime Plan Exclusive Bonus:
- **Free "Done-For-You" Website Setup (Worth ₹5,000):**
  - Our team will manually upload up to **50 Products** or **150 SKUs**.
  - Basic logo placement and theme color setup.

---

## 2. PREMIUM PLAN (The "Reels" Edition - Most Popular)
*Target Audience:* Established D2C brands, fashion influencers, and sellers who want a modern, high-converting "Instagram-like" shopping experience.

### Pricing Options:
- **Monthly Subscription:** ₹1,999 / month
- **Lifetime Ownership:** ₹29,999 (One-time) + ₹4,000 / year (Server & Maintenance Fee)

### Order Limits & Commission:
- **Free Orders:** Up to 2,000 Orders per month are absolutely FREE (0% Commission).
- **Post-Limit Commission:** 1.5% flat commission on every order after the first 2,000 orders in a month.

### Features Included:
- **Everything in Basic, PLUS:**
- **Video Commerce / Reels Feature:** Shoppable short videos for high conversion.
- Custom Domain Setup (e.g., *www.yourbrand.com*).
- Advanced Analytics & Sales Dashboard.
- Abandoned Cart Recovery (Email Automation).

### 🎁 Lifetime Plan Exclusive Bonus:
- **Free "Premium" Website Setup (Worth ₹10,000):**
  - Our team will manually upload up to **200 Products** or **600 SKUs**.
  - Custom design and upload of 2-3 High-Quality Website Banners.
  - Complete Category & Collection routing setup.

---

## 3. ENTERPRISE PLAN (The "Quick Commerce" Edition)
*Target Audience:* Dark stores, grocery chains, restaurants, and massive sellers needing hyperlocal Blinkit/Zepto-like infrastructure.

### Pricing Options:
- **Monthly Subscription:** ₹3,999 / month
- **Lifetime Ownership:** ₹49,999 (One-time) + ₹7,500 / year (Server & Maintenance Fee)

### Order Limits & Commission:
- **Free Orders:** Up to 5,000 Orders per month are absolutely FREE (0% Commission).
- **Post-Limit Commission:** 1% flat commission on every order after the first 5,000 orders in a month.

### Features Included:
- **Everything in Premium, PLUS:**
- **Quick Commerce Module:** Hyperlocal routing, delivery radius limits, and pin-code blocking.
- Multi-Staff Accounts (Give limited access to managers/delivery boys).
- Dedicated Priority WhatsApp Support.
- Zero OTP Cost (Platform provides free SMS OTPs up to a limit).

### 🎁 Lifetime Plan Exclusive Bonus:
- **Free "VIP White-Glove" Setup (Worth ₹20,000):**
  - Our team will manually upload up to **500 Products** or **1,500 SKUs**.
  - Setup and tagging for the first 5-10 Shoppable Reels.
  - Delivery Radius Map mapping.
  - Dedicated Onboarding Manager via a 1-on-1 Zoom Training Call.

## Core Business Logic Summary
1. **The Server-Cost Shield:** If a store goes viral, they will easily cross their "Free Orders" limit. The 1% - 2% commission triggered after the limit will automatically cover all increased Server, Bandwidth (CDN), and OTP API costs.
2. **The Lifetime Upsell:** The "Free Setup" hooks make the Lifetime plans incredibly attractive. A user looking to pay ₹999/month will seriously consider paying ₹15k just to avoid the headache of uploading their catalog themselves.
3. **The Maintenance Fee:** The yearly maintenance fee (₹2.5k to ₹7.5k) on Lifetime plans ensures the platform always has recurring capital to pay Vercel and Supabase server bills, preventing a scenario where lifetime users become a financial burden in year 2 or 3.
