# Project Quotation & Detailed Scope of Work
**For: E-commerce & Quick Commerce Platform (Blinkit-Style UI/UX)**
**Date:** 10 July 2026

## 1. Executive Summary
This quotation outlines the technical deliverables, exhaustive scope of work, and commercial terms for the development and deployment of a full-scale E-commerce and Quick-Commerce (Q-commerce) ecosystem. The platform will feature an exact Blinkit-style UI/UX, built for high performance, scalability, and seamless user experience across all devices and stakeholders.

## 2. Scope of Work (Deliverables)
The project includes the development and handover of the following dedicated systems:

### 2.1. Customer-Facing Interfaces (Blinkit Exact UI/UX)
- **Web Application (PWA & Desktop):** Fully responsive web platform optimized for SEO and lightning-fast load times.
- **Android Application:** Native-feel Android app for customers available on the Play Store.
- **iOS Application:** High-performance iOS app for Apple users available on the App Store.

### 2.2. Admin & Management Interfaces
- **Admin Web Dashboard:** A comprehensive desktop/web control panel to manage the entire business operations.
- **Admin Mobile App:** A dedicated Android/iOS app for store owners and managers to monitor sales, inventory, and approve orders on the go.

### 2.3. Logistics & Partner Interfaces
- **Rider (Delivery) App (Android/iOS):** Dedicated application for delivery agents featuring live GPS routing, order assignment, earning history, and delivery proofing (OTP/Photo).
- **Affiliate / Partner App & Web Portal:** System for affiliates and influencers to track referrals, generate tracking links, and manage commission payouts.

---

## 3. Comprehensive Feature List
The system will be equipped with the following state-of-the-art features:

### 3.1. E-Commerce & Quick-Commerce Core
- **Dual Mode Switch:** Seamless toggle between standard delivery (E-commerce) and instant 10-minute delivery (Quick-commerce).
- **Dynamic Category Navigation:** Blinkit-style sidebar and grid layout for rapid category browsing.
- **Variant Management:** Multi-variant product support (Size, Weight, Color) with dynamic pricing.
- **Smart Search & Filters:** Instant predictive search with filters for price, brand, and categories.
- **Live Inventory Tracking:** Real-time stock sync to prevent overselling.

### 3.2. Advanced Admin & Business Operations
- **Dynamic UI Builder:** Change homepage banners, hero sections, and category layouts directly from the admin panel without coding.
- **Bulk Upload System:** Import thousands of products, coupons, or customers via Excel/CSV instantly.
- **AI-Powered Assistant:** Built-in AI to generate product descriptions, SEO tags, and marketing copy.
- **Order Management System (OMS):** Kanban-style order tracking (Pending, Dispatched, Delivered, Cancelled).

### 3.3. Marketing & Customer Retention
- **Advanced Coupon Engine:** Create percentage-based, flat discount, and BOGO (Buy-One-Get-One) promo codes.
- **Flash Sales & Timed Deals:** Countdown timers and automatic discount applications for specific time windows.
- **Product Reviews & Ratings:** Verified customer review system with admin moderation.
- **Wallet & Loyalty System:** In-app digital wallet for cashbacks and refunds.

### 3.4. Technical & Security Features
- **Row Level Security (RLS):** Military-grade database security ensuring strict data isolation.
- **Multi-Location Inventory:** Route orders to the nearest warehouse for faster delivery.
- **Payment Gateway Integrations:** UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, NetBanking, and COD support.
- **Automated Invoicing:** Auto-generation of GST-compliant invoices and order receipts.
