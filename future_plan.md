# 🚀 Future Expansion Roadmap (ERP Portal — Phase 2 & Beyond)

Yeh document un advanced modules aur enterprise features ko track karta hai jo core MVP (Phase 1) ke baad release kiye jayenge.

---

## 1. 📚 Library Management System (Digital LMS)
*School ki physical library ko fully automate karne ke liye.*
- **Book Cataloging:** ISBN/Barcode scanning se nayi books ka record banana.
- **Issue & Return Engine:** Student/Teacher ke ID card barcode ko scan karke 5 second me book issue/return.
- **Overdue Fine Calculation:** Return date cross hone par automatically per-day fine calculate hona aur student ke "Fee Dues" me link ho jana.
- **Digital Library / E-Books:** PDF notes, question banks aur sample papers upload karne ka repository.
- **Parent View:** Parents dashboard me dekh sakte hain ki bacche ne kaunsi books issue karayi hain aur kab return karni hain.

---

## 2. 📦 Inventory & Store Management (Uniforms, Books & Stationery)
*School ke internal store aur asset purchase/sales ko manage karne ke liye.*
- **Stock Tracking:** Uniforms (sizes wise), books sets, aur lab equipments ka realtime stock counter.
- **Low Stock Alerts:** Stock khatam hone se pehle accountant/storekeeper ko notification.
- **POS / Counter Billing:** Parent jab counter par uniform ya books lene aaye, to instantly thermal printer se formatted GST receipt nikalna.
- **Vendor & Purchase Orders:** Vendors se aayi supply ke invoices aur payments ka ledger maintain karna.

---

## 3. 💵 HR & Payroll Management (Staff Salaries & Leaves)
*School teachers aur non-teaching staff ki complete HR cycle.*
- **Automated Salary Slip:** Staff ki biometric/online attendance aur approved leaves ke hisaab se monthly net salary calculate karna.
- **Deductions & Benefits:** PF, ESI, TDS, aur Advance Loan deductions ki automated entry.
- **1-Click Payslip Download:** Teachers apne dashboard se direct monthly salary slip PDF download kar sakte hain.
- **Direct Bank Transfer Export:** Bank format (Excel/CSV) me payout file export karna taaki 1-click me bulk salary transfer ho sake.

---

## 4. 🛡️ Visitor & Smart Gate Security Management
*School campus me safe entry & exit workflow.*
- **Guard Tablet App:** Main gate par guard visitor ki live photo lega, phone number lega aur purpose select karega.
- **Host Staff Approval:** Visitor kisse milna chahta hai (e.g., Principal ya Class Teacher) — unke phone par instant "Approve/Reject" notification.
- **Early Pickup OTP:** Agar koi parent bacche ko chhutti se pehle lene aaye, to registered parent mobile par OTP aayega. OTP verify hone par hi bacche ko gate se bahar bheja jayega.

---

## 5. 🎓 Alumni Network & Career Cell
*Passed-out students ka record aur interaction.*
- **Alumni Directory:** Passed out batches ka database, unke current higher education/jobs ka record.
- **Transcript & Document Requests:** Alumni portal se online duplicate marksheet ya recommendation letter apply karna.
